Welcome to the course's interactive digital book. Here you will find all course-related content, along with exercises, help, and additional resources.

Grading System

  • Individual Deliverables 25%: These will consist of biweekly assignments to practice the course content.
  • Theory Quizzes 10%: Short quizzes to review the most theoretical content.
  • Group Project 65%: Design and develop a multiplayer video game using the course content. There will be monthly presentations/devlogs to monitor progress.

You must score at least a 5 in all sections to pass the course.

Use of AI tools

AI may be used throughout the course, although it is not recommended so you can solidify your knowledge much better. Furthermore, the instructor may require an in-person defense, either through a presentation or a short interview, for any of the aforementioned assignments.
